💰 Expense Tracker

🇬🇧 English

A simple and lightweight expense tracking application built with Flutter.

The app helps users manage their daily income and expenses, organize financial transactions by category, and keep track of their spending.

Features:

- Add income and expenses.
- Organize transactions into categories.
- View recent financial transactions.
- Filter transactions by type.
- Light and Dark Mode.
- Choose your preferred currency.
- Simple and user-friendly interface.

Technologies & Tools:

- Flutter & Dart – App development.
- SharedPreferences – Local data storage on the device.
- Material Design – User interface.
- ValueNotifier – State management and UI updates.
